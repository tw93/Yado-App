# Yado

English · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [Deutsch](README.de.md) · [Français](README.fr.md) · [Español](README.es.md) · [Italiano](README.it.md)

Official guides and feedback for **Yado**, a native Mac app for managing an iPhone from the Mac: clear duplicate photos, large videos and unused apps, arrange the Home Screen, move files and keep backups on your own Mac.

Yado is a paid app, and each purchase funds its development and updates. This repository is for guides, questions and feedback. It doesn't contain the app's source code.

## Get Yado

| Requirements | Download | User guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel, and an iPhone | [yado.house](https://yado.house/?utm_source=github.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=yado-app-readme) | [Help](https://yado.house/help?utm_source=github.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=yado-app-readme) |

Yado is created and maintained by [Tw93](https://github.com/tw93), and works over a cable or, once paired, over the same Wi-Fi. No jailbreak, Apple ID sign-in or app on the phone is needed.

| Tab | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | See battery health, the watts it is charging at, what fills the storage and whether the cable is fast enough, and take screenshots framed in the phone |
| Clean | Review duplicates, large videos, blurry photos and clutter such as receipts and parking spots in one place, each confirmed before anything leaves the phone |
| Photos | Browse by album or by place, export originals to the Mac, and keep the best of each group of similar photos |
| Apps | Find apps unopened for weeks, gone from the App Store or not updated in a year, uninstall them, and sort the Home Screen into folders |
| Files | Drag files between the iPhone and the Mac |
| Backups | Back up on a schedule to the Mac or an external drive, read contacts, notes and messages one by one, and restore to the same or another iPhone |

### Start

1. Download Yado from the website above, drag it into Applications and open it.
2. Connect the iPhone with a cable, unlock it and tap Trust. To connect over Wi-Fi later, choose Connect over Wi-Fi in the Yado menu, the swallow at the left of the tab bar.
3. Every action that changes the phone works twice for free before you buy, while looking, scanning, browsing backups and making backups stay free. After buying, open Settings, click Manage beside Yado License on the General tab, paste the key from the Dodo Payments email and click Activate Yado.

## FAQ

### Does a public GitHub repository mean Yado is open source?

No. This repository holds documentation and community feedback, and the app's source code is private.

### How much is Yado?

$19 USD once for two Macs, with no subscription and free updates. Within 14 days of buying, email [hi@yado.house](mailto:hi@yado.house) from the address you paid with for a full refund.

### Could Yado delete the wrong photos?

A deleted photo goes to Recently Deleted on the iPhone, where it can be restored for 30 days. Photos that Clean moves to the Mac leave the phone only after the copy is checked as the full original, and every group of duplicates starts with its best photo kept.

### Does Yado go online, and what does it send?

Photos, files and backups never leave your Mac. Yado goes online only to download the app category list and Apple's list of iOS versions, check your license, look for updates and, if you agree after a crash, send a crash report. The apps on your phone are never sent. The [privacy notes](https://yado.house/privacy?utm_source=github.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=yado-app-readme) have the details.

### Where is my license key, and how do I move it to a new Mac?

Dodo Payments emails the key to the address entered at checkout; search your mailbox and spam for Dodo. If it isn't there, email [hi@yado.house](mailto:hi@yado.house) from the address you paid with, or with the Payment ID, and keep these details out of public issues. One license works on two Macs: when both seats are taken, Yado lists them on the new Mac, and deactivating one gives the new Mac its seat.

### Which website is official?

**yado.house** is Yado's only website. "Yado: Claude Code & Codex" on the App Store is an unrelated app from another developer.
